OilPrice.com | July 18, 2026 · 1 min read

That system kept the lights on while the rest of the island waited close to a year for power. It also became the seed for one of the country's most closely watched community microgrids. Nine years and several storms later, microgrids have moved well past the disaster-recovery story.
