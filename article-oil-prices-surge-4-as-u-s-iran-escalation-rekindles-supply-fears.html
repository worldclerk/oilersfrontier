Oil Prices Surge 4% as U.S.-Iran Escalation Rekindles Supply Fears

allies in the region. Brent Crude futures surged by 4% to top $79 per barrel in Asian trading, to the highest level in more than three weeks, as the market fears this weekend’s hostilities may not subside as easily as in previous short-term instances of flare-ups.
